What the original DFW program involves

The original free template by Geoff Neupert alternates kettlebell clean-and-press sets with front-squat sets in 30-minute sessions, three times per week. It progresses the rep pattern across four weeks and has a fifth-week transition. It emphasizes staying fresh rather than grinding reps.

DFW is a separate kettlebell program built around the clean-and-press and front squat. Learn the movements and choose suitable weights before trying it. A qualified coach can help assess technique and readiness.

03 — FOOD THAT SUPPORTS TRAINING

Build meals.
Skip the food rules.

Build meals around familiar foods you enjoy. Examples include dal, vegetables, eggs, yogurt, tofu or paneer, rice, and roti. Protein shakes can be convenient, but they do not have to replace meals.

04 — SUPPLEMENTS, WITHOUT THE HYPE

Food and training come first.

Supplements are optional. The right choice depends on your health, diet, and medicines.

01◒

Protein powder

A convenient food option if it helps. Many people can get enough protein from meals and snacks.

OPTIONAL CONVENIENCE
02✳

Creatine

Creatine monohydrate has evidence for some repeated, intense efforts. Benefits vary; it is not a must-have.
